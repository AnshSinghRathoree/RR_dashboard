How it works · no maths degree needed

🌡️

1 · Pressure Meter

Before every ball we ask: how tough is this moment? Big target left, wickets falling, last overs, a knockout game? Each ball gets a score from 0 (relaxed) to 1 (Super Over).

🔥

2 · Clutch Boost

We take the hottest 25% of balls and compare each player with himself. A batter who scores faster, or a bowler who leaks fewer runs, when it matters, earns a high Clutch Index.

🛒

3 · Buyable Players Only

Players already kept by their teams, retired, or unavailable are removed. Every name below is released or unsigned for the 16 Dec Goa auction.

Method in one box
Batter Clutch = 50 + 15 × [.33·RAPP + .25·ΔRAPP + .10·Boundary% + .10·ΔDot% + .10·Balls/Dismissal + .07·Consistency + .05·IPL-proven] × N/(N+160)
Bowler Clutch uses runs saved, wickets per spell, dot+boundary balls, and k = 180. Weights add to 100%.
Pool funnel: batters 118 → 43 · bowlers 176 → 68 after removing ineligible, retired and already-retained players (142 of the 182 league-wide retained/traded players were in our qualified pool).
Pressure score ≥ 0.364 = "high-pressure" (hottest 25% of balls). Needs ≥ 100 high-pressure balls to qualify.
